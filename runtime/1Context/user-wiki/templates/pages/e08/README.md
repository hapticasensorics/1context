# e08 Page Templates

These page templates are derived from the e08 For You experiment structure, but
they are not copies of filled pages.

They represent the blank forms those pages should have had before agents wrote
content: frontmatter placeholders, stable sections, and explicit empty markers.
They are not active wiki source and do not decide the final public-launch
sitemap. Promote a page into `user-wiki/source/families/` only after the page
family and route are reviewed.
